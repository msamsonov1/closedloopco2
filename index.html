Supercritical Fluid Decontamination & Transport Physics

Mathematical formulation of high-density extraction, organophosphorus ligand chelation, and non-destructive porous aggregate leaching.

1. Thermodynamic Density & Peng-Robinson EOS

Supercritical carbon dioxide operates above its thermodynamic critical juncture ($T_c = 304.25\text{ K}$, $P_c = 7.38\text{ MPa}$). At high pressures ($>20\text{ MPa}$), $sc\text{CO}_2$ reaches liquid-like solvating densities while retaining near-gas diffusivity, enabling full penetration of concrete micro-pores.

$$P = \frac{RT}{v - b} - \frac{a(T)}{v(v + b) + b(v - b)}$$

Where $a(T) = 0.45724 \frac{R^2 T_c^2}{P_c} \alpha(T)$ and $b = 0.07780 \frac{R T_c}{P_c}$. Solvation power scales non-linearly with fluid density $\rho$:

$$\ln S = \ln S_0 + \frac{k_B T \cdot \rho}{\kappa_T} + \Delta v^\ddagger \frac{(P - P_c)}{RT}$$

By maintaining $\rho > 750\text{ kg/m}^3$, the dissolution equilibrium shifts four orders of magnitude relative to subcritical gas phases.

2. Ligand Chelation Coordination Equilibrium

Actinide surrogate cations ($\text{UO}_2^{2+}$, $\text{Ce}^{4+}$, $\text{Eu}^{3+}$) are ionic and insoluble in non-polar neat $\text{CO}_2$. Doping the fluid with organophosphorus complexes (TBP: tri-$n$-butyl phosphate) and fluorinated $\beta$-diketones (HTTA) encapsulates metal charges inside lipophilic envelopes:

$$\text{UO}_2^{2+}{}_{(\text{pore})} + 2\text{NO}_3^- + 2\text{TBP}_{(\text{sc})} \rightleftharpoons \text{UO}_2(\text{NO}_3)_2(\text{TBP})_2{}_{(\text{sc})}$$

The multi-phase partition coefficient $K_D$ governs total extraction efficiency across pore surface boundaries:

$$K_D = \frac{[\text{Actinide}]_{\text{fluid}}}{[\text{Actinide}]_{\text{matrix}}} = K_{\text{ext}} \cdot [\text{TBP}]^2_{\text{sc}} \cdot [\text{NO}_3^-]^2_{\text{eff}}$$

Hydrophobic fluorinated shells shield the coordinate core, enabling neutral dissolution directly into supercritical continuous media.

3. Intra-Pore Knudsen & Molecular Mass Transfer

Unlike viscous aqueous acids that exhibit severe boundary layer resistance in fine micro-cracks ($<50\text{ nm}$), $sc\text{CO}_2$ exhibits zero surface tension and an effective diffusion coefficient $D_{\text{eff}}$ three orders higher than liquid water:

$$\frac{1}{D_{\text{eff}}} = \frac{\tau}{\varepsilon} \left( \frac{1}{D_{\text{bulk}}} + \frac{1}{D_{\text{Knudsen}}} \right), \quad D_{\text{Knudsen}} = \frac{d_{\text{pore}}}{3} \sqrt{\frac{8 R T}{\pi M}}$$

The 1D transient radial leaching within concrete aggregate rubble is modeled via Fick's Second Law with interfacial desorption sink terms:

$$\frac{\partial C_s}{\partial t} = \frac{D_{\text{eff}}}{r^2} \frac{\partial}{\partial r}\left( r^2 \frac{\partial C_s}{\partial r} \right) - k_{\text{desorp}}(C_s - C_{\text{sat}})$$

This achieves deep extraction without mechanical crushing beyond coarse 0.5-inch gravel sizes.

4. Real-Time Observer Inversion & Closed-Loop Loopback

Downstream dry precipitation is achieved entirely via controlled isenthalpic flash depressurization ($\Delta P < P_c$). As solvating power collapses, heavy complexes drop out as dry crystals while $100\%$ of $\text{CO}_2$ and ligands are recycled:

$$\hat{x}_{k+1} = \mathbf{A}\hat{x}_k + \mathbf{B}u_k + \mathbf{L}\Big(y_k - \mathbf{C}\hat{x}_k\Big), \quad u_k = -\mathbf{K}\hat{x}_k$$

The observer estimates real-time fluid density $\hat{\rho}$ and aggregate boundary clearance from high-speed in-situ acoustic time-of-flight and pressure transducers, preventing inadvertent subcritical aerosol carryover.

$$\text{Clearance Verification: } \int_0^{t_{\text{cycle}}} \dot{m}_{\text{chel}} \cdot dt \ge M_{\text{initial}} \times 0.95$$

Guarantees verifiable compliance before batch autoclave decompression and free-release disposal.
